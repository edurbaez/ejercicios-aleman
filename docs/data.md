# Vocabulary data files

Detail doc split out of the root `CLAUDE.md` (which keeps a one-line index entry per file). Keep this file in sync per the root Maintenance rule.

## Data

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `DataA1.json` | Vocabulary data for A1 (~900 entries, 16 lists). **Hand-curated, not generated by `scripts/generate-vocab.js`** — don't regenerate it with that script. Source: the official *Goethe-Zertifikat A1 / Start Deutsch 1 Wortliste* (covers ~87% of its ~684 entries; the rest are derivable forms) plus standard A1 textbook vocabulary (classroom, body, animals). Core lists keep their names because `plan.js`/`chatvoz2`/`lectura veloz` read them by key: `esenciales` 62 (numbers, pronouns, question words, prepositions, time adverbs), `verbos` 134, `sustantivos` 142 (people/family, home, school, nature, city, health, body — days/months moved to `tema: hora y calendario`), `adjetivos` 94 (colors as adjectives, not nouns), `expresiones` 81. Extra lists: `verbos separables`, `palabras funcionales` (conjunctions, particles, possessives, place adverbs) and 9 `tema: …` lists (comida y bebida, ropa y compras, transporte y viajes, datos personales, trabajo y servicios, clase, hora y calendario, vivienda, tiempo libre). Spanish translations are kept unique within each list so the 4-option quiz is never ambiguous (e.g. `mieten`/`vermieten`, `gewinnen`/`verdienen`). A1 headwords were removed from `DataA2/B1/B2/C2.json`. |
| `DataA2.json` | Vocabulary data for A2 (~710 entries, 18 lists). **Hand-curated, not generated by `scripts/generate-vocab.js`** — don't regenerate it with that script: the previous GPT-generated version repeated ~230 A1 words, its `esenciales` duplicated the other lists and `expresiones` was 100 padded `zum …` variants. Source: the official *Goethe-Zertifikat A2 Wortliste* (headwords not already in `DataA1.json`, plus its Wortgruppen: school subjects, holidays) and standard A2 textbook vocabulary; the *Netzwerk neu A2* Kapitelwortschatz is a scanned PDF (no text). Core lists keep their names because `plan.js`/`chatvoz2`/`lectura veloz` read them by key: `esenciales` 52 (adverbs, connectors `weil`/`dass`/`wenn`/`deshalb`, indefinites), `verbos` 92, `adjetivos` 86, `sustantivos` 74 (abstract/general), `expresiones` 74 (A2 Redemittel: opinion, proposals, letters, wishes). Extra lists: `verbos separables`, `verbos reflexivos` and 11 `tema: …` lists (salud y cuerpo, escuela y estudios, trabajo y dinero, medios e internet, ocio y cultura, tráfico y viajes, vivienda y hogar, comida y cocina, tiempo y naturaleza, fiestas y familia, ropa y accesorios). No headword repeats A1; A2 headwords were removed from `DataB1/B2/C2.json` (homonyms with another sense kept: `das Gericht` in B2, `führen` in C2; `das Stipendium` stays in C1). Seed with `node scripts/seed-word-lists.js a2`. |
| `DataB1.json` | Vocabulary data for B1 (~1120 entries, 22 lists). **Hand-curated, not generated by `scripts/generate-vocab.js`** — don't regenerate it with that script: the previous GPT-generated version had overlapping lists and wrong translations (`sich verhalten`="comportarse" inside `expresiones`, `esenciales` duplicating `verbos`). Source: the official *Goethe-Zertifikat B1 Wortliste* (https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/de/Goethe-Zertifikat_B1_Wortliste.pdf, alphabetical part parsed per column with pdfplumber), filtered against `DataA1/A2.json` and the Goethe A1/A2 Wortlisten, plus B1 Redemittel (Goethe B1 Sprechen/Schreiben) for `expresiones`. Core lists keep their names because `plan.js`/`chatvoz2`/`lectura veloz` read them by key: `esenciales` 70 (adverbs/particles), `verbos` 172, `sustantivos` 133 (abstract/general), `adjetivos` 137, `expresiones` 72 (opinion, planning, presentation and letter Redemittel plus common idioms). Extra lists: `verbos separables`, `verbos reflexivos`, `verbos-prep` (with case, same format as C1), `conectores` and 13 `tema: …` lists (trabajo y empresa, estudios y formación, trámites y oficinas, banco y dinero, salud y medicina, medios y tecnología, sociedad y política, delitos y justicia, tráfico y transporte, naturaleza y medio ambiente, cultura y ocio, vivienda y hogar, comida y compras). Spanish translations unique within each list. No headword repeats A1/A2/C1 (collisions stayed in those levels); B1 headwords were removed from `DataB2/C2.json`. Seed with `node scripts/seed-word-lists.js b1`. |
| `DataB2.json` | Vocabulary data for B2 (~1050 entries, 21 lists). **Hand-curated, not generated by `scripts/generate-vocab.js`** — don't regenerate it with that script. Source: the public Klett *Aspekte neu B2* Kapitelwortschatz (~600 entries picked from it, filtered against A1/A2/B1/C1) completed with standard Goethe/telc B2 vocabulary (most of `conectores`/`verbos-prep`/`nomen-verb`, the `expresiones` Redemittel). Core lists keep their names because `plan.js`/`chatvoz2`/`lectura veloz` read them by key: `esenciales` 41, `verbos` 120, `sustantivos` 126, `adjetivos` 172, `expresiones` 84. Extra lists: `conectores` (conjunctions + genitive/dative prepositions, as in C1), `verbos-prep`, `nomen-verb`, `verbos separables` and 12 `tema: …` lists. Spanish translations unique within each list; no headword repeats another level (B2↔C2 overlaps resolved 2026-10-01, rarer items like `erachten`/`binnen`/`ungeachtet` kept in C2). Used by `B2.html`. Seed with `node scripts/seed-word-lists.js b2`. |
| `DataC1.json` | Vocabulary data for C1 (~1040 entries, 18 lists). **Hand-curated, not generated by `scripts/generate-vocab.js`** — don't regenerate it with that script: the previous GPT-generated version had translations shifted by one position (62/91 adjetivos, 34 verbos, 21 sustantivos), a padded `expresiones` (44× `im Zuge der Markt…`) and an `esenciales` that duplicated the other lists. Source: the public *Aspekte neu C1* word lists (Klett: Kapitelwortschatz + Alphabetische Wortliste), filtered against A1–B2 and C2. Core lists keep their original names (`esenciales` 50, `verbos` 126, `sustantivos` 154, `adjetivos` 142 — adjectives and adverbs, `expresiones` 96 — idioms and Redemittel) because `plan.js` links them via `?set=` and `chatvoz2`/`lectura veloz`/`marketing` read them by key. Extra lists: `nomen-verb` (66 Funktionsverbgefüge, aligned with the C1 grammar rule), `verbos-prep` (67, with case), `conectores` (48 connectors + Genitive/Dative prepositions) and 10 thematic lists `tema: …` following the book's chapters (rutina, lenguaje, trabajo, economía, metas, salud, derecho, identidad, arte, memoria). No German headword repeats within C1 or with any other level. Seeded to Supabase with `node scripts/seed-word-lists.js c1`. |
| `DataC2.json` | Vocabulary data for C2 (~950 entries, 21 lists). **Hand-curated, not generated by `scripts/generate-vocab.js`** — don't regenerate it with that script: the previous GPT version padded `expresiones` (`ungeachtet der …` ×32). No public C2 word list exists (*Erkundungen C2*, Schubert, ships none — only its chapter themes were used); curated from C2 knowledge. Core lists keep their names: `esenciales` 48 (elevated adverbs), `verbos` 112, `sustantivos` 124, `adjetivos` 106, `expresiones` 88 (idioms). Extra lists: `nomen-verb`, `verbos-prep`, `conectores` and 13 `tema: …` lists (ciencia, filosofía, derecho, economía, política, medios, literatura, artes plásticas, psicología, medio ambiente, sociedad, cuerpo humano, animales en sentido figurado). Spanish translations unique within each list; no headword repeats another level. Seed with `node scripts/seed-word-lists.js c2`. |
